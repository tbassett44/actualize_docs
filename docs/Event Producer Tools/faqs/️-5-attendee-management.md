**How do I check in attendees at the event?**

Navigate to Admin Tools -> Checkin. It will allow you to search Ticket Sales for individuals or scan their QR code to check them in. 

You can delegate ticket-checking scanners to others in the app by going to Admin Tools -> Ticket Settings -> Settings. Click the + button under "Users to Give Check-in Access." When delegated scanners view the event page, they will see a pop-up allowing them to check people in.

**Can I view or export my attendee list?**

Yes! On mobile, you can view your attendee list after the event by navigating to Admin Tools → Check-In.

On a desktop, you can also export your attendee list by clicking the Tools button on the Check-In page. From there, you can download your list as a CSV or PDF file for your records.

**How do I communicate updates to attendees?**

Navigate to Admin Tools -> Contact RSVPs -> Message to send communications to them in the app. You can filter by ticket holders, rsvp'd but haven't purchased, rsvp'd going, or rsvp'd interested.

**What’s the best way to handle last-minute cancellations or no-shows?**

Actualize does not manage attendee cancellations or no-shows directly. By default, tickets are non-refundable, but event hosts may choose to offer refunds or credits at their own discretion. We recommend clearly stating your event’s cancellation policy in the description so attendees know what to expect.

**How can I verify attendees’ tickets?**

Go to Admin Tools → Check-In to manage attendance. From there, you can scan attendees’ QR codes or search for their names or email addresses within your Ticket Sales list to check them in quickly and accurately.

**Can I add attendees manually?**

Yes, navigate to Admin Tools -> Orders, then click "Tools" in the top-right corner to manually add to the guest list. 

**How do I resend or reissue tickets?**

Navigate to Admin Tools → Orders, then click the three dots next to a guest’s ticket purchase to view additional options. From there, you can resend or reissue their tickets as needed.

**Can I limit attendance to verified community members only?**

At this time, you can’t restrict events to verified community members only. However, you can set a password for specific ticket types or offer discounted tickets only to verified community members to help control access.
