**What is a 1099-K and when will I receive one?**

A 1099-K is a tax form issued by Stripe that reports the total payment transactions you’ve processed through the platform during the year. You’ll receive your 1099-K by January 31 of the following year, delivered directly through Stripe Express.

**What’s the minimum earnings threshold for 1099-K reporting?**

Stripe will issue a 1099-K if your total payments exceed $5,000 and you’ve had more than 200 transactions in a calendar year (thresholds may vary by state).

**How do I update my tax information (W-9, EIN, etc.)?**

You can update your tax details directly in Stripe Express. Log in using the same email associated with your Actualize account and follow the prompts to edit or verify your information.

**Are taxes automatically deducted, or do I pay them separately?**

Taxes are not automatically deducted. You’re responsible for reporting and paying any applicable taxes on your event income separately.

**Will international producers receive different forms?**

No. Tax forms are based on where the event takes place, and we currently only support events hosted within the United States.

**What should I do if my tax form information is incorrect?**

If your 1099-K contains incorrect information, log in to Stripe Express to update your tax details or correct any errors.

**How do I report income from multiple events?**

All your earnings from multiple events are automatically combined and reported together on your 1099-K, based on your total payouts for the year.

**Does the platform collect and remit taxes on my behalf?**

No. Actualize does not collect or remit taxes for event hosts. We issue a 1099-K through Stripe to report your total payment volume for the year.
