**How can I track ticket sales and attendance data?**

After you create your event, you can navigate to Admin Tools to view the features we provide. The Dashboard will provide an overview of revenue, ticket sales, and tickets remaining. You can also go to Orders to get more information about ticket sales/orders. You can navigate to Payouts to view more information about revenue, outstanding payments, and more. Navigate to Checkin to view attendance data.

**Do I get insights into demographics or referral sources?**

Demographic data isn’t available at this time. However, you can view referral source insights on **desktop** by going to Admin Tools → Affiliates. This section shows where your ticket sales and traffic are coming from.

**How can I promote my event within the Actualize Earth ecosystem?**

Go to Admin Tools → Spread the Word, then click Promote!

The Promote feature highlights your event within the Actualize Earth ecosystem—such as featured spots or visibility at the top of relevant pages.  
The Invite feature, on the other hand, lets you send direct invitations to specific people.

Both tools allow you to select a location to help target the right audience for your event.

**Can I connect my event to social media platforms?**

You may use the event page URL (available after clicking the "Share" button on the event page) to link to your event on other social media platforms. You may also link your social media platforms under Descriptions in your event page.

**How do affiliate links or referral programs work?**

Affiliate tracking is available on the **desktop version** of the platform. Navigate to Admin Tools → Affiliates to create and manage affiliate links. These links let you track where your event traffic and ticket sales are coming from, making it easy to measure the impact of your referrals and partnerships.

**Can I embed ticket widgets on my own website?**

After creating your event, navigate to your event's page. Under the main image, there is a "Share" button for a link and a QR code button. You may embed either into your website, however you see fit.

**Can I integrate with email marketing or CRM tools?**

Not at this time. Actualize doesn’t currently support direct integrations with external email marketing or CRM platforms.
