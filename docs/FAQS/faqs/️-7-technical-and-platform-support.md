**What should I do if my event isn’t publishing or if there’s a technical issue during ticket sales?**

If you experience any technical issues—whether your event isn’t publishing, loading correctly, or there’s a problem during ticket sales—you can report it directly through the platform.

Click your profile picture → Bug/Upset Reports to submit the issue, or go to Admin Tools → Support to chat with our AI assistant or use the Contact Us button.

You can also reach us directly at [team@actualize.earth](mailto:team@actualize.earth)

**How do I update event details after publishing?**

Navigate to Admin Tools -> Event Settings -> Edit Event.

**How do I manage events from the mobile app vs. desktop?**

In both, you manage events from Admin Tools under an Event's Page. Your events page is available under your profile.

**Can I clone an event and adjust details easily?**

Yes! Click your event to go to the Event Page, then toggle to Admin Tools at the bottom. Under Event Settings, you may clone your event!
