**How do I create ticket types (General Admission, VIP, Early Bird, etc.)?**

After you create your event, you can navigate to Admin Tools -> Ticket Settings -> Edit Tickets. From there, you may click the "+ Add Ticket!" and create new ticket types!

**Can I offer free or donation-based tickets?**

Yes! After you create your event, you can navigate to Admin Tools -> Ticket Settings -> Edit Tickets. From there, you may click the "+ Add Ticket!" and edit ticket types, including free/donation-based.

**Can I set tiered pricing or limit ticket quantities?**

Yes! After you create your event, you can navigate to Admin Tools -> Ticket Settings -> Edit Tickets. From there, you may click the "+ Add Ticket!" and edit ticket types, including pricing and ticket quantities.

**How do promo codes or discount links work?**

After you create your event, you can navigate to Admin Tools -> Discounts. From there, you may click the "+ Add Discount!" There are several settings to choose from, so the discount is ideal for your event.

**Can attendees transfer their tickets to others?**

Ticket transfers aren’t currently supported within the platform. However, attendees are welcome to make their own arrangements directly with the event host if they need to give their spot to someone else.

**How do I track ticket sales and remaining tickets available?**

After you create your event, you can navigate to Admin Tools to view the features we provide. The Dashboard will provide an overview of revenue, ticket sales, and remaining tickets. You can also go to Orders to get more information about ticket sales/orders. You can navigate to Payouts to view more information about revenue, outstanding payments, and more.

**How do refunds and cancellations work?**

To cancel an event, go to Admin Tools → Event Settings → Cancel Event. If tickets were purchased, Actualize will process refunds through Stripe, our secure payment platform.

Please note that Stripe and platform fees apply to each ticket sale. In the case of refunds, the event host is responsible for covering those fees. Events cannot be canceled until all ticket sales have been fully refunded.

**How do I collect attendee information?**

While we don’t yet support custom fields or waiver forms, you can access attendee details through your Ticket Orders. From there, go to Tools to export your orders as a PDF or CSV, or export just the names and emails as a CSV (**available on desktop only**).

**Can I sell tickets at the door?**

Yes!  We support Tap to Pay on Android and iOS.  This is available in the ticket checkin tool.  If your device supports Tap to Pay, you will see a button in the bottom left that says "sell tickets".  If you have not yet registered your device for Tap to Pay, you will be onboarded, and then you will have access to use the Tap to Pay to receive in person ticket sales.

**Do I have to use Actualize's ticketing system to list an event?**

No! You can list an event on Actualize without using the built-in ticketing system. You can also add a link to an external ticketing site (like a venue's box office) directly on your event page.
