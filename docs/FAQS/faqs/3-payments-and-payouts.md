**What payment methods are supported (credit card, Apple Pay, crypto, etc.)?**

We currently accept Credit Card and Debit Card payments through Stripe.  We are about to release a new app version that enables the tap-to-pay receiving ability on people's phones.  We are also actively investigating economic systems and cryptocurrencies that could serve as payment or payout methods.

**When and how do I get paid for my ticket sales?**

Ticket purchases typically take 3 days for their funds to clear.  Once these funds have been marked clear, they become available for you to pay out. You can do incremental payouts when you like.  *note* to issue refunds to someone, if you do not have enough money in your holdings to cover the refund, you have to add money to pay for the cost of the refunds. 

**Is there a payout schedule or threshold?**

Payouts happen when issued by an event admin for an event.  Once it is sent, Stripe clears bank accounts once every 24 hours.

**What are the platform fees and how are they calculated?**

Platform fees are $0.99 per ticket and $0.50 per order. If you have a lifetime membership, the $0.99 per-ticket fee is waived—you’ll only pay the $0.50 per-order fee.

**Can I split payouts among multiple collaborators or payees?**

Yes! Navigate to Admin Tools -> Payout. Press "+ Add Payout," then select a person or a page. Under Payout Type, you can choose how to split the payout —by a fixed amount, a percentage, or as an expense.

**What's the difference between expenses and payouts?**

Expenses are costs related to running your event, such as food, venue rental, and other supplies. They’re used for accounting and tax purposes (for example, they can be deducted when filing your 1099-K).

Payouts, on the other hand, are payments made to people working at your event—like door staff, musicians, or kitchen crews.

**How do I connect my bank account or payment method?**

We conduct our payments through Stripe. Click your profile picture, then select "Bank Settings" to connect your payment methods. 

You can also navigate to Admin Tools -> Payout. There is an area to connect or edit your bank settings at the top of the page. 

**How can I track my revenue and payouts in real time?**

After you create your event, you can navigate to Admin Tools to view the features we provide. The Dashboard will give an overview of revenue, ticket sales, and related metrics. You can also go to Orders to get more information about ticket sales/orders. You can navigate to Payouts to view more information about revenue, outstanding payments, and more.

**What happens if a chargeback or dispute occurs?**

If a chargeback or payment dispute occurs, Actualize handles the process directly through Stripe, our secure payment processor. We’ll review the case and communicate with the necessary parties to resolve the issue as smoothly as possible.

**How do I issue refunds to attendees?**

To cancel an event, navigate to Admin Tools -> Event Settings -> Cancel Event. If tickets were purchased, Actualize will issue refunds to holders via Stripe (a secure online payment processing platform).

Please note that Stripe and platform fees apply to each ticket sale. In the case of refunds, the event host is responsible for covering those fees. Events cannot be canceled until all ticket sales have been fully refunded.
